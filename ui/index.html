Fire risk is based on satellite fire detections from the last 12 months. Satellites can miss fires under clouds or haze. This is a risk screening, not proof.

Forest loss is detected from satellite maps with 30 m pixels. Small clearings, or clearings hidden by clouds, can be missed. Tree loss is not always deforestation, so a red result needs a closer look.

RSPO: international certificate for sustainable palm oil. ISPO: Indonesia's national sustainability certificate.
